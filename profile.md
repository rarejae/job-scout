  forward deployed engineer, applied AI engineer, AI solutions engineer,
  agent engineer, AI enablement / value creation roles.
- Environment over title: sanctioned agentic tooling, API access, freedom to
  build internal automations as an IC.
- PE/VC-adjacent seats where AI + data work drives portfolio or deal value.
- Escapes the billable-hours model entirely.
- Startups or product companies where he'd ship, not advise.

## What scores LOW (0-4)

- AI as cosmetic garnish on a traditional analyst/PM/admin job.
- Pure research scientist roles requiring a PhD.
- Heavy people-management with no build component.
- Roles requiring 5+ years in a niche he doesn't have (e.g., pure infra/SRE).
- Locations requiring relocation outside Chicago/NYC with no remote option.

## Seniority calibration
~2-3 years experience plus founder-level shipping ability. Titles from
"engineer/associate" through "senior" are fine. Staff/principal/director
requiring 7+ years: score down unless the posting says experience is flexible.
